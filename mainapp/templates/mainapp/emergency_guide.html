What to Do in an Emergency

1

Stay Calm

Take a deep breath and assess the situation.

2

Ensure Safety

Move to a safe place and check for any immediate danger.

3

Call Emergency Services

Contact local emergency services immediately.

4

Provide Details

Share your location and full details of the situation.

5

Follow Guidance

Follow official instructions and wait for assistance.

Safety Tips for Travelers

  • Keep digital & physical copies of important documents.
  • Share your itinerary with family or friends.
  • Avoid unsafe areas and follow local warnings.
  • Keep emergency contacts saved in your phone.
  • Always have a backup of your travel insurance.
